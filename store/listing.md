## App content (Policy → App content)

- **Ads:** No, the app contains no ads. Change this when AdMob goes in.
- **App access:** everything is available without an account or special access.
- **Content rating (IARC questionnaire):** category Game. No violence (the dog catches squirrels; nothing is hurt), no fear, sexual content, bad language, drugs, or gambling. Players can't talk to each other or share information. No purchases yet. Expect Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Target audience:** the game is cartoon animals, so Google will treat it as appealing to kids whatever you pick. The honest choice is to include the under-13 age groups, which puts the app in the Families program. It already fits: no ads, no personal data, generated names only, and a privacy policy. When ads arrive they must come from a Families-certified SDK (AdMob qualifies) with child-directed settings.
- **Data safety:**
  - Collected: **App activity → Other actions** (game scores). It is sent to our server after each round, along with the generated nickname and the dog and squirrel played, and shown on the public leaderboard. Purpose: app functionality. Not shared with third parties (AWS processes it for us as a service provider). Encrypted in transit: yes. Players can't opt out of score submission.
  - Not collected: name, email, location, contacts, photos, device or advertising IDs, analytics. The nickname is generated by the game and isn't tied to a person, so it isn't a "User ID".
  - Account deletion URL: not needed, because the app has no accounts.
- **Government, financial, health, news:** none apply.

## Closed test (personal accounts made after Nov 2023)

1. Testing → Closed testing → create a track and add at least 12 testers by email list.
2. Upload `android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab` (build it with `npm run android:bundle`) and accept Google-managed app signing. Our key only uploads.
3. Roll out, then send testers the opt-in link. They must stay opted in for 14 days in a row.
4. After 14 days with 12+ testers, apply for production access from the Dashboard.
