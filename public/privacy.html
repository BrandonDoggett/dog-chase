Dog Chase! Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Dog Chase! is a game made by eldoggo software. It's built to collect as little as possible: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves your leaderboard nickname, your best score, and your dog, squirrel, and world choices in the app's (or your browser's) local storage. Clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data deletes all of it.

What we send to our server

When you finish a round, the game sends your nickname, your score, the dog and squirrel you played, and a one-time game token to our leaderboard server, hosted by Amazon Web Services in the United States. Your nickname, score, dog, squirrel, and the date are shown publicly on the leaderboard.

Nicknames are picked at random by the game from a fixed list of words (like "SwiftCorgi42"). Players can't type a name or any other text, so the leaderboard can't hold anyone's real name.

What we don't collect

We don't collect names, email addresses, locations, contacts, photos, or device or advertising identifiers. The game has no ads and uses no analytics or cookies. Our hosting provider handles technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the game, and we don't use it to identify players.

Children

Dog Chase! is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

How long we keep data

Leaderboard entries are kept so the daily, weekly, and all-time rankings work. One-time game tokens are deleted automatically shortly after use.

Removing a leaderboard entry

Email us the nickname and date of the score, and we'll remove it.

Security

Everything the game sends travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
